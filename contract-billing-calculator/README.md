# Contract Billing Calculator & Invoice Review

An Excel practice portfolio project simulating contract-based invoice calculations, invoice reconciliation, and profitability reporting. The workbook and data are **fictional/simulated**, created to demonstrate practical billing and Excel skills.

## What this project demonstrates

- Billing calculations for **Fixed Price**, **Cost Plus**, and **Time & Materials** contracts
- Contract detail retrieval with **XLOOKUP**
- Labor, materials, and internal-cost rollups with **SUMIFS**
- Automated reconciliation of entered invoices against expected amounts
- Overbilling, underbilling, and correct-invoice status flags using **IF** and conditional formatting
- Gross profit, gross margin, and an overall billing summary
